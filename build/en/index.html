Recognition & Enforcement

Your German divorce is not automatically valid in Turkey. We handle recognition and enforcement of German divorce decisions in Turkey — fully from Germany, without travel.

Family Law & Divorce

Turkish-origin families across Germany often face a dual challenge with divorce and maintenance: two legal systems, two countries, one family. Turkish family law differs significantly from German law and requires a lawyer who understands both sides.

Inheritance Law

Inherited property, land or bank accounts in Turkey? We manage Turkish inheritance law matters from Germany, including certificate of inheritance, rejection deadlines, tax and land registry transfer.

Criminal Law

Expert criminal defence and legal advice in Turkish criminal proceedings — directly from Mannheim. Whether it's a summons, arrest warrant or Interpol notice: we guide you through the Turkish criminal justice system.
